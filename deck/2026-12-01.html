DiscoveryDec 1

Value proposition

Problem

  • Drones hide behind buildings, out of radar and camera view
  • Track reacquired by hand under time pressure
  • Lost track means longer lockdowns
  • Visible sensors need host-nation approval

Solution

  • Acoustic nodes hear rotors around corners
  • Roof radar, cameras and RF fill the rest
  • ROAREE fuses every sensor into one track
  • Track cues cameras and goes to TAK

Value

  • Continuous track through occlusion
  • Shorter, better-targeted shelter-in-place
  • Small passive nodes, ~$125 each
  • Works with sensors bases already own

DiscoveryDec 1

COL Huston's feedback

Open architecture

Don't depend on specific sensors or models. Swap or add parts without a redesign.

Track custody

When a drone leaves camera or radar view, acoustic sensors keep the track.

Single track

The operator needs the same drone on one track, regardless of which sensor reports it.

Prototype scope

Exact accuracy is not the goal; parts can be simulated.

Design rationale

Why each part was chosen and how it would be improved.

Priority

Track continuity across sensors. The other limits depend on it.

Node pipeline

1Listen4 mics combined for a stronger signal
2CleanFilter wind rumble and hum
3FFTSound into frequencies, 15 per second
4CombEvenly spaced peaks: the rotor signature
5ConfirmPattern holds over several frames
6AlertPitch, loudness, confidence over LoRa

Generator: fixed comb. Traffic and wind: no comb. Drone: comb shifts with throttle. Breadboard layouts
