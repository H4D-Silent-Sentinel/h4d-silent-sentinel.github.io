Counter-UAS
sensor fusion

Acoustic, radar, EO/IR (electro-optical/infrared) and RF (radio frequency) sensors fused into a common operating picture.

Beyond
line of sight

Group 1 UAS (unmanned aircraft systems) at low altitude in urban terrain are masked from radar and EO/IR by structures. Detections from all sensors feed one Kalman filter. Target identity is maintained through coverage gaps.

Radar detects the target. TRK-01 opens.

The structure blocks radar line of sight. TRK-01 continues on its last estimate.

Acoustic nodes detect the rotors and update TRK-01. Sound does not need line of sight.

Radar detects the target again. TRK-01 keeps its identifier and history.

Command and control

Edge fusion

The C2 (command and control) node runs on one laptop at the operator position. It localizes acoustic detections, fuses all sensor reports, cues sensors to the predicted position and publishes to TAK (Team Awareness Kit) as CoT (Cursor on Target).

Sensor modalities

  2. 02Radar Node

01

Acoustic Node

4 ICS-43434 MEMS (micro-electro-mechanical systems) microphones on an ESP32-S3. Detects rotor blade-pass harmonics. Position from relative received level across nodes. Passive.

Link
LoRa 915 MHz
Nodes
4, one per corner
02

Radar Node

RD-03D 24 GHz radar on an ESP32-S3 bridge. Range, azimuth and radial velocity of moving targets.

Link
UDP (User Datagram Protocol) 47012
Coverage
±60° azimuth, ~8 m bench
03

Camera Node

YOLO11 (You Only Look Once) detection, ByteTrack association and VLM (vision-language model) confirmation on a Jetson Orin Nano. Reports bearing and label.

Link
UDP 47010
Compute
Jetson Orin Nano
04

RF Node

ADALM-Pluto SDR (software-defined radio). Detects 2.4 and 5.8 GHz control and video links. Passive.
