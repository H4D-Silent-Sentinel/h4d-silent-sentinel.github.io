C-UAS · Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems

Silent Sentinel

Passive acoustic tracking of small drones beyond radar and camera line of sight.

System → Latest briefing
Capability

Track custody beyond line of sight

Group 1 UAS (unmanned aircraft systems) drop out of radar and camera coverage behind buildings and at low altitude. Acoustic nodes hold the track until another sensor reacquires.

Detect

Passive acoustic detection

Four MEMS (micro-electro-mechanical systems) microphones per node detect rotor blade-pass harmonics. Receive-only.

Locate

Loudness localization

Position from relative sound level across nodes. Reports over LoRa (Long Range) radio, 915 MHz.

Hold

Multi-sensor fusion

Acoustic, radar and camera reports fuse into one Kalman track. The track number persists when any one sensor loses the drone.

Cue

Sensor re-cueing

Predicted position cues cameras, the chaser drone and nodes. Tracks export to TAK (Team Awareness Kit) as CoT (Cursor on Target).
